# Wetland Bird Notes

Field notes from the Te Awa wetland restoration area.

In 2018, observers recorded kereru feeding near the western kahikatea stand. Tui were also seen around flowering harakeke. Volunteers noted that bird activity increased after pest control work began.

In 2020, a community survey mentioned pukeko, riroriro, and kotare near the boardwalk. The notes recommend further monitoring during spring.
